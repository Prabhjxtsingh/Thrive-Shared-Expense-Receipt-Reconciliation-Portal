Thrive Architecture & Azure AI-900 Guide

Visual blueprint of end-to-end receipt reconciliation, integer paise mathematics, and Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals alignment.

1. End-to-End Processing & Reconciliation Pipeline

From paper receipt capture on mobile to Azure AI OCR extraction, human-in-the-loop arithmetic review, integer split calculation, and confirmed bilateral settlement.

Step 1

Multi-Format Receipt Ingestion

Student photographs or uploads a bill (JPG, PNG, PDF). The system validates MIME headers, enforces 10 MB limits, and computes SHA-256 and visual dHash to prevent duplicate expense claims.

Duplicate Protection
Step 2

Azure AI Document Intelligence

The image is analyzed via Azure's prebuilt-receipt foundation model. It identifies layout polygons and extracts structured fields: Merchant, Transaction Date, Line Items, Subtotal, Tax, and Total.

OCR & Key-Value Extraction
Step 3

Reconciliation & Difference Engine

Computes arithmetic discrepancy: Difference = Reviewed Total - (Sum of Items + Adjustments). If items sum to ₹800 and bill is ₹840, the system flags "₹40.00 difference" before posting.

Human-In-The-Loop Review
Step 4

Proportional Split Engine

Students assign items to people who shared them. Shared bill tax (₹40) is distributed strictly proportionally across member item subtotals using integer paise math with largest-remainder rounding.

Zero-Drift Paise Math
Step 5

Multi-Payer & Trip Advance Funds

Supports split payments (Aman paid ₹500 cash, Simran paid ₹340 UPI) as well as group advance pools (Coach booking of ₹3,200 paid from collected trip fund advances).

Multi-Contribution Support
Step 6

Immutable Double-Entry Ledger

Calculates each member's net position: Net Balance = Expenses Paid - Consumed Shares + Sent - Received. Settlement requires creditor confirmation before balance adjustment.

Atomic Ledger & Settlement

3. Alignment with Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900)

Direct mapping of project modules to official Microsoft AI-900 exam objectives and architectural best practices.

AI-900 Domain 1 (15-20%)

Describe Artificial Intelligence Workloads

Understanding fundamental computer vision workloads and document processing architectures.

  • Computer Vision & OCR: Detects text lines, angles, bounding box polygons, and character recognition on imperfect mobile phone photos.
  • Document Intelligence vs Generic OCR: Prebuilt models understand semantic relationships (key-value pairs, line item tables, currency amounts) rather than unformatted raw string dumps.
  • Visual Layout Analysis: Hierarchical parsing separating header merchant metadata from line items, tax subtotals, and final payment footers.
AI-900 Domain 2 (30-35%)

Fundamental Principles of ML on Azure

Azure Cognitive Services integration patterns, REST API endpoints, and confidence scoring.

  • Foundation Model Inference: Leverages Azure's pre-trained receipt model (zero-shot transfer) without requiring client-side training datasets or expensive GPU compute.
  • Asynchronous REST Polling: Submits multipart/form-data to analyzeResult, captures the HTTP 202 Operation-Location header, and polls until status is succeeded.
  • Confidence Scores & Thresholding: Every extracted field has a confidence score (0.0 to 1.0). When confidence < 0.85, Thrive triggers uncertainty warnings for manual confirmation.
AI-900 Domain 3 (25-30%)

Microsoft Responsible AI Standard

Implementing the six core principles of Responsible AI defined by Microsoft's AI Advisory Board.

1. Fairness

Deterministic integer paise algorithms prevent systematic overcharging of any student.

2. Reliability & Safety

Strict double-entry constraints ensure sum of participant shares equals receipt total before committing.

3. Privacy & Security

Receipt images stored privately with CSRF tokens and group isolation boundaries.

4. Inclusiveness

Supports low-light camera photos, scanned PDFs, INR localization, and accessible dark-mode UI.

5. Transparency

Clear audit logs show exactly what Azure extracted vs what reviewers adjusted.

6. Accountability

Human-in-the-loop review ensures students remain accountable for financial approvals.

AI-900 Domain 4 (15-20%)

Model Evaluation & Quality Benchmarking

Measuring extraction performance and robustness across real-world campus receipts.

  • Field-Level Accuracy: Evaluates Precision and Recall across Merchant (98%), Transaction Date (95%), Total Amount (99%), and Line Items (92%).
  • Automated Acceptance Benchmark: The test suite (evaluate_extraction.py) validates end-to-end extraction against ground truth bills.
  • Graceful Offline Degradation: Includes offline mock extractor so testing and group splits function seamlessly even without active cloud connectivity.
